---
name: Race review privacy
description: Which local experiment history can be shared in a classroom boat review, and when.
---

Only include saved experiments matching the submitted boat design in the shared post-race review. Treat submitted histories as participant-provided measurements, not server-verified physics. Freeze the boat and its submitted history when the competition begins; do not expose the full editable design or history to peers before the race finishes. The race does display each actual boat's visible shape, material finish, and installed parts as requested; treat those observable features as public, but bucket numerical proportions instead of exposing precise editable settings. Comments are unattributed publicly, but posting requires proof that the device belongs to a participating group or the room host.

**Why:** A browser's local experiment history can contain tests from other boats or other students on a shared device. Sending the entire history would disclose unrelated work and misleadingly attribute it to the submitted boat. Hiding detailed numeric design until the finish prevents premature inspection, while showing the real boat lets the class observe meaningful differences during the race. Requiring a private room capability for posting keeps a broadly shared QR link from becoming an unrestricted anonymous comment endpoint without attributing comments to authors.

**How to apply:** If adding broader history sharing or rematches, require an explicit scope choice and label it clearly to readers. Keep results separate from comments and avoid turning public review endpoints into editing paths.
