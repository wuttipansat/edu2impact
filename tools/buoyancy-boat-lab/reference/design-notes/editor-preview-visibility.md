---
name: Editor and preview visibility
description: Why boat controls should remain near the simulation rather than extending the entire page.
---

Keep the boat-building controls in a height-limited, internally scrollable frame beside the simulation on wide screens. On narrow screens, keep the stacked control frame short enough that the simulation is nearby rather than below the full list of settings.

**Why:** Students need to observe the boat while changing inputs; a full-height form pushed the simulation too far away to see during data collection.

**How to apply:** New boat settings belong inside the existing scrollable controls without increasing the frame to fit all content. Preserve keyboard access to the scroll area and keep the simulation visible or close by across responsive layouts.
